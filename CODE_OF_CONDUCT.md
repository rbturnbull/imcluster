# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Be considerate, welcome constructive disagreement, and focus feedback on the
work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, and disclosure of
private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to robert.turnbull@unimelb.edu.au. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.
